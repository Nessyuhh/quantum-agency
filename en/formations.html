Training

Your teams self-sufficient with AI

Three levels, on your premises, on your own files. No demo: by the end, your teams know how to do it, and know what not to do.

01

Level 1 · 1 day

Discover

Understand what AI does well, what it does badly, and use it from the next day on.

Who it is for

Executives, managers and teams who have never really used AI, or use it without a framework.

Format

On your premises, 6 to 12 people, computers provided by you.

By the end of the day, you will know how to

  • Write an e-mail, meeting notes or a proposal in a few minutes, then proofread it methodically.
  • Ask AI a good question, and recognise a bad answer.
  • Know which information must never be pasted into a consumer tool.

On the programme

  • What an AI model is, in ten minutes and without equations.
  • Hands-on with Claude, ChatGPT and Gemini on your real cases.
  • One use case per job function present in the room.
  • Data security rules, written together, to post on the wall.
02

Level 2 · 2 days · recommended

Automate

Build your own first automatic flow, on a task you actually do.

Who it is for

The people who do the repetitive tasks today: admin, sales, accounting, human resources.

Format

On your premises, 4 to 8 people, on your real tools and real documents.

By the end of the day, you will know how to

  • Spot in your day what can run on its own, and what must stay manual.
  • Assemble a flow with an automation tool (n8n or Make), and run it.
  • Create a custom AI assistant for a precise task, with your documents.

On the programme

  • Day 1: the basics of automation, and a first simple flow built by each participant.
  • Day 2: workshop on your own files, your own flow, ready to use on Monday.
  • How to monitor what runs on its own, and what to do when it gets it wrong.
03

Level 3 · tailored

Steer

Decide, budget and frame the company's AI projects, without depending on a specialist.

Who it is for

Executive committees, transformation leads, project managers.

Format

Half a day to two days depending on your needs, for management and project leads.

By the end of the day, you will know how to

  • Sort projects: what pays off, what costs, what can wait.
  • Read a quote or a vendor proposal, and ask the right questions.
  • Apply data protection rules and the European AI Act to your situation.

On the programme

  • A twelve-month roadmap, built during the session on your priorities.
  • Internal framework: who may do what, with which tools, with which data.
  • One-to-one executive coaching, optional, after the session.
Our training courses are run with our certified training partners. We help you put together the funding application with your training body, when your situation allows it.
