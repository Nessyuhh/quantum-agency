Services

What we do, concretely

Four ways to help you. All of them start from the same point: a free audit of the way you work, jargon-free and with no commitment.

01

A free audit, and a free showcase website

Thirty minutes to spot what costs you time. You leave with three priorities and a website to get started.

Who it is for

Any SME or mid-sized company wondering where to start with AI, without knowing what is realistic.

Duration

30-minute conversation, summary within 48 h.

What changes

You finally know what is worth automating in your company, what is not, and in what order. No hundred-page report: three written priorities, quantified in time saved, that you can launch or not.

How it works

  • A thirty-minute conversation, by video call or on your premises, with one of our consultants.
  • We walk through a typical day: what repeats, what blocks, what gets done twice.
  • Within forty-eight hours you receive a one-page summary: three priorities, what they bring, what they require.
Free showcase website

We give a simple showcase website to the SMEs and mid-sized companies that get in touch: your business, your services, how to reach you. Online, under your name, and yours to keep. No conditions, whether you take the audit or not.

02

Automate what repeats

Invoices, reminders, quotes, meeting notes: these tasks run on their own, with your current software.

Who it is for

Companies where hours go every week into data entry, copy-paste and manual reminders.

Duration

2 to 6 weeks for a first flow in service.

What changes

A flow triggers itself: an invoice arrives by e-mail, it is read, checked, entered into your accounting software, and the supplier is notified. You only step in when something is out of the ordinary. We build these flows with automation tools (n8n, Make) and the most suitable AI models, without changing your software.

How it works

  • Together we pick a first flow, the one that pays off fastest.
  • We build it and test it on your real documents, with the person who does the task today.
  • We put it into service, train that person to monitor it, and stay reachable.
03

Plug AI into your tools

Your existing software (quotes, customers, documents) becomes able to read, summarise and write.

Who it is for

Companies that already have good tools (sales management, accounting, shared documents) and want them to do more.

Duration

1 to 3 months depending on the number of tools.

What changes

Your teams ask your documents a question and get a sourced answer. A quote is drafted from a customer request. Meeting notes are generated from a recording. All inside the software your teams already know: nothing new to learn.

How it works

  • We look at what your tools can already connect to.
  • We pick the AI model suited to each use, including models hosted in France when your data requires it.
  • We connect, we test with your teams, we document what was done.
04

Advise you over time

One contact who follows your projects, arbitrates investments and keeps you away from dead ends.

Who it is for

Leaders who want to move forward over twelve months without hiring a full-time specialist.

Duration

12-month engagement, monthly meetings.

What changes

You have a clear roadmap: what to launch, in what order, with what budget. You know what the regulations say (data protection, the European AI Act) and what they mean for you. And you have someone to call before signing a quote or a subscription.

How it works

  • A monthly meeting with management, on progress and decisions to make.
  • A twelve-month roadmap, reviewed every quarter.
  • An independent opinion on tools and vendors: we sell no software.
