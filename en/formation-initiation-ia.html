Why train your teams in AI now ?

Artificial intelligence is no longer a technology of the future. In 2025, 73% of employees in France already use AI tools in their work often without training or framework. The result: siloed uses, missed productivity gains and ignored risks (GDPR, hallucinations, data confidentiality).

Our corporate AI initiation training answers a simple question: how to move from a naive and dispersed use to a collective, framed and immediately productive mastery? In one day, your collaborators (sales, assistants, managers or executives) leave with methods and tools they can use the next day.

This is not a theoretical training on "what is AI". It's a practical workshop focused on results, calibrated to the realities of your profession.

What the training covers

The training alternates between targeted theory and intensive practice, with exercises adapted to your sector at each stage. The precise schedule is organized by the training partner in charge of your session.

Module 1

Understanding AI without technical jargon

How ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini really work. Key differences between models. What AI can and cannot do. Classic pitfalls to avoid (hallucinations, bias, sensitive data). Introduction to GDPR best practices.

Module 2

Mastering the prompt: the art of speaking to AI

The difference between a basic question and an effective prompt. The CRAC structure (Context, Role, Action, Constraints). Prompt writing exercises tailored to the participants' needs. Creation of reusable templates for your team's recurring tasks.

Module 3

Practical AI use cases according to your profession

Writing (emails, reports, presentations, commercial offers). Research and information synthesis. Data analysis and tables. Preparation of meetings and client appointments. Idea generation and brainstorming. Each exercise is adapted to your sector

Module 4

Applied workshop: your real topics

Participants test AI on their own daily tasks. Collective construction of a team prompt library. Questions and answers.

✦ What your teams take away

A prompt guide adapted to your industry, usable immediately. A concrete mastery of essential AI tools. And above all: the methods and confidence to integrate AI into their daily routine in a structured and productive way.

Administrative & management profiles

Frequently Asked Questions about AI initiation training

Do our collaborators need to create accounts on ChatGPT ?

A free ChatGPT account is sufficient for most exercises. However, we recommend the Claude Pro or ChatGPT Plus subscription (€20/month) for an optimal experience. The investment is recovered in a few hours of saved work.

Can we do the training for a team of 3 people only?

Yes. We adapt the format for small groups from 3 participants. The dynamic is different but often more intense, the exercises are even more personalized.

What is the difference with an online training (MOOC) on AI ?

A generic MOOC does not adapt exercises to your sector, does not answer your specific questions, and does not create the group dynamic that anchors habits. Face-to-face training generates concrete uses that your team continues to apply after the session, with an application rate 4 to 5 times higher.
