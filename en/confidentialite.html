Privacy

Your data, in plain words

What we collect on this site, why, for how long, and how you can say no. In everyday language, no surprises.

Who we are

This site is published by Quantum Consulting (QC), a French single-shareholder simplified joint-stock company (SASU) registered under number 982 259 038, with its registered office at 229 rue Saint-Honoré, 75001 Paris. We are the data controller for everything described here. Questions: contact@quantum-agency.fr.

What we collect, and why

The audit form. When you request an audit, we receive your name, your e-mail address, your company name and, if you filled it in, your message. We use them to reply to you and prepare the conversation, nothing else. They reach us by e-mail, through a sending service (Resend) and a technical relay (Cloudflare), then stay in our mailbox. We keep them for three years after our last exchange, then delete them.

The free showcase website offer. If you leave your e-mail address in that window, we use it to send you the details and to answer you. One e-mail, no mailing list, no sharing. Same retention period.

Technical logs. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages and served through Cloudflare. Like any server, they briefly record technical information (IP address, page requested, browser) for security and reliability. We do not use them to track anyone.

Audience measurement

We use Google Analytics to learn which pages are read and where our visitors come from. It helps us improve the site. In practice:

  • Without your consent, no cookie is stored on your device. Google only receives anonymous technical signals, with no identifier, from which it estimates overall traffic. You cannot be identified.
  • If you accept, Google Analytics stores cookies that tell your browser apart from another one, for thirteen months at most. Your IP address is truncated before being recorded.
  • We enable neither personalised advertising nor data sharing with other Google services.

You can change your mind at any time: change my choice. Your answer is remembered for six months, in your browser's local storage, without any cookie.

Your rights

You can access the data we hold about you, have it corrected or deleted, object to its processing, or ask for a copy. An e-mail to contact@quantum-agency.fr is enough; we answer within a month. If you believe we have not respected your rights, you can contact the French data protection authority, the CNIL (cnil.fr).

What we do not do

  • We never sell or rent your data to anyone.
  • We do not send newsletters you did not ask for.
  • We do not follow your browsing on other websites.
